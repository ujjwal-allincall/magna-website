About Us

Welcome to Magna Healthcare & Research Centre

A Unit of Bhaskar Foundation

Magna Healthcare & Research Centre is a hospital opened by Dr. Ravi Bhaskar, providing comprehensive care to patients suffering from lung-related diseases. We are dedicated to offering the best treatment for respiratory conditions in Lucknow.

Our center is engaged in research programs in the field of pulmonology and conducts regular lung awareness talks and seminars. We provide excellent consultation for individuals of all age groups with a focus on advanced diagnostic and therapeutic techniques.

About Dr. Ravi Bhaskar

Dr. Ravi Bhaskar is one of the top Interventional Pulmonologists in India, recognized for his revolutionary work in the Pulmonary Medicine domain. He emphasizes the use of advanced diagnostic and therapeutic techniques for patients with lung airway disorders and pleural diseases.

Dr. Ravi Bhaskar, the visionary behind Magna Healthcare & Research Centre, is known as one of the most renowned pulmonologists in Lucknow for his great experience and expertise in Pulmonology care and critical care.
