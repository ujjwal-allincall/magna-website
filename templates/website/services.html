Our Services

Comprehensive Healthcare Services for Respiratory and Lung Disorders

Magna Healthcare & Research Centre provides a comprehensive range of services for the diagnosis, treatment, and management of respiratory and lung-related conditions. Our services are designed to meet the needs of patients of all age groups.

Consultation

We provide excellent consultation in Lucknow for individuals of all age groups. Our expert team offers comprehensive evaluation and personalized treatment plans for various respiratory conditions. Patients are invited to discuss their concerns and receive expert medical advice.

  • General Pulmonology Consultation
  • Pediatric Respiratory Care
  • Geriatric Respiratory Care
  • Follow-up Consultations

Second Opinion

A second opinion is a valuable resource when you are faced with difficult health circumstances or choices regarding your treatment. We provide expert second opinions to help you make informed decisions about your healthcare.

  • Expert Review of Medical Reports
  • Treatment Plan Evaluation
  • Alternative Treatment Options
  • Comprehensive Medical Assessment

Nebulization

A nebulization treatment involves the use of a nebulizer that converts liquid medicine into a mist or vapor, allowing it to be inhaled directly into the lungs. This is particularly effective for treating asthma, COPD, and other respiratory conditions.

  • Immediate Relief for Breathing Difficulties
  • Effective Medication Delivery
  • Safe for All Age Groups
  • Supervised Administration

Lung Function Test

Pulmonary function testing helps assess how well your lungs are working. These tests measure lung capacity, airflow, and gas exchange to diagnose and monitor respiratory conditions.

  • Spirometry
  • Lung Volume Measurement
  • Diffusion Capacity Testing
  • Exercise Testing

Pulse Oximetry

Pulse oximetry is a non-invasive method to monitor the oxygen saturation level in your blood. This simple test helps assess respiratory function and oxygen levels.

  • Non-invasive Testing
  • Quick Results
  • Continuous Monitoring Available
  • Essential for Respiratory Assessment

Bronchoscopy

A bronchoscopy is a test that allows a doctor to examine your airways. Your doctor will thread an instrument called a bronchoscope through your nose or mouth, down your throat, and into your lungs to visualize the airways and diagnose conditions.

  • Diagnostic Bronchoscopy
  • Therapeutic Procedures
  • Biopsy Collection
  • Foreign Body Removal

Thoracoscopy

Thoracoscopy is a minimally invasive procedure that allows visualization of the pleural space and lung surface. It is used for both diagnostic and therapeutic purposes.

  • Pleural Disease Diagnosis
  • Pleural Biopsy
  • Pleurodesis
  • Minimally Invasive Approach

Intercostal Chest Drainage

Intercostal chest drainage is a procedure to remove air, fluid, or blood from the pleural space. This is essential for treating conditions like pneumothorax, pleural effusion, and hemothorax.

  • Pneumothorax Treatment
  • Pleural Effusion Management
  • Emergency Care
  • Expert Procedure

Sleep Study

Sleep studies help diagnose sleep disorders, particularly sleep apnea. These tests monitor your breathing, heart rate, and other body functions during sleep.

  • Polysomnography
  • Sleep Apnea Diagnosis
  • Sleep Disorder Evaluation
  • Treatment Planning

Allergy Test

Comprehensive allergy testing helps identify allergens that may be causing respiratory symptoms. This is crucial for effective allergy management and treatment.

  • Skin Prick Testing
  • Blood Allergy Tests
  • Allergen Identification
  • Treatment Recommendations

CPAP | BIPAP Therapy

Continuous Positive Airway Pressure (CPAP) and Bilevel Positive Airway Pressure (BIPAP) therapy are effective treatments for sleep apnea and other breathing disorders during sleep.

  • Sleep Apnea Treatment
  • Device Fitting & Setup
  • Ongoing Support & Monitoring
  • Improved Sleep Quality
