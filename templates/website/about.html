About Dr. Ravi Bhaskar

Consultant Chest Physician and Interventional Pulmonologist

Dr. Ravi Bhaskar is one of the top Interventional Pulmonologists in India, recognized for his revolutionary work in the Pulmonary Medicine domain. He emphasizes the use of advanced diagnostic and therapeutic techniques for patients with lung airway disorders and pleural diseases.

Dr. Ravi Bhaskar, the visionary behind Magna Healthcare & Research Centre, is known as one of the most renowned pulmonologists in Lucknow for his extensive experience and expertise in Pulmonology care and critical care.

Located in the heart of Lucknow, our center is easily accessible, patient-friendly, and cost-efficient for all your respiratory problems. Dr. Bhaskar's approach combines clinical excellence with personalized care, ensuring that each patient receives the attention and treatment they deserve.

About Magna Healthcare & Research Centre

Magna Healthcare & Research Centre (A Unit of Bhaskar Foundation) is a hospital opened by Dr. Ravi Bhaskar, providing comprehensive care to patients suffering from lung-related diseases. We are the first full-time dedicated pulmonary healthcare center in the heart of Lucknow city.

Our center is committed to providing the best treatment for respiratory conditions, including asthma, COPD, sleep disorders, and other lung-related ailments. We combine clinical excellence with compassionate care to ensure the best outcomes for our patients.

Our Mission

To provide world-class healthcare services in pulmonology and respiratory medicine, making advanced diagnostic and therapeutic techniques accessible to all patients in Lucknow and surrounding areas.

Our Vision

To be the leading center for pulmonary healthcare and research in Northern India, recognized for excellence in patient care, innovation in treatment, and commitment to medical education and awareness.
